Our story

Why we started

Ansu Edu Support Hub was established on 1 August 2026 by Jaffar Ansari. The idea came from something we kept seeing: capable students losing out on admissions, scholarships and jobs not because they weren't qualified, but because the process itself was confusing, rushed or simply out of reach.

We didn't want to be just another service counter. Our aim is to guide each person through their educational and professional journey, and stay with them from the beginning of a task until it's completed.

The problems we set out to solve

  • Difficulty completing university admission forms
  • Little information about scholarships and internships
  • Problems submitting online job applications
  • Limited knowledge of professional CVs and cover letters
  • Struggling to prepare assignments and presentations
  • No access to computer and digital skills training
  • No time to join separate academies or institutes
  • Trouble accessing national & international online courses
  • No individual mentorship or professional guidance
  • Extra challenges faced by students with disabilities
  • Hard to find home tuition and suitable teachers
